# 2026-03-29 — MuJoCo Warp Go2 Environment

## What happened

Built the Go2 Warp environment end-to-end. Trains on unitree_mujoco's exact go2.xml (full cylinder collision geometry) via the MuJoCo Warp backend. This eliminates the sim2sim gap caused by MJX's missing cylinder-box collisions.

## Key decisions

- **New env class, not modifying existing MJX env** — clean separation, zero risk to working MJX path. Warp is Beta in Playground.
- **Vendored unitree_mujoco's go2.xml** (NOT Menagerie's) — this is the actual deploy model. Menagerie go2.xml has different damping (2.0 vs 0.1), friction (0.6 vs 0.4), and is tuned for MJX stability rather than hardware accuracy.
- **Two contact modes** — `contact_mode="training"` (firm Go1-style contacts, proven for gaits) and `contact_mode="deploy"` (unitree-native physics, for transfer validation).
- **No actuator remapping** — policy trains from scratch on unitree's FR-first actuator order. Deploy code already handles remapping at inference time.
- **Controlled duplication** of go2_joystick.py → go2_warp_joystick.py. Will likely diverge during reward/physics tuning. Shared sensor helpers extracted to go2_sensors.py to avoid duplicating that layer.

## Research finding

Asymmetric actor-critic was invented OFF-policy (Pinto et al. 2017, DDPG), not on-policy. ICML 2025 paper proves unbiased policy gradients for any algorithm. No published system combines SAC + privileged critic + legged locomotion — added as future TODO.

## Results

- **Full run (50M steps, 1024 envs):** 91k sps with naccdmax=2000. In progress.

## Issue: CCD overflow

At 1024 envs, Warp's CCD buffer was too small for unitree's complex collision geometry. Produced 8.6M lines of overflow warnings, dropped sps from 91k → 65k. Fixed by setting `naccdmax=2000`. Remaining ~1430 warnings are occasional spikes. See lessons/mjx.md for details.

## Debugging trail

1. **CCD overflow spam** — 8.6M warning lines. Fixed: naccdmax=4000, ccd_iterations=100, njmax=100.
2. **No eval output** — `--eval-every` is episodes not steps. 5M episodes never reached.
3. **Joints contorting** — forcerange=[0,0] in unitree XML = unlimited torque. Fixed by setting forcerange=ctrlrange.
4. **Still low eval (14.1 vs MJX 244)** — solver settings differ massively: iterations=100 vs 1, elliptic vs pyramidal cone, eulerdamp enabled vs disabled.
5. **record_video.py crash** — unitree XML has no named cameras. Fixed with free camera + body tracking fallback.
6. **PD hold test** — zero-action ragdoll showed physics is fine (both MJX and Warp collapse identically). PD hold (Kp=35, Kd=0.1) showed calves losing against gravity: pos_error grew monotonically → oscillation spike at step 51 → collapse.
7. **Root cause: PD gains** — Kd=0.1 is 5x too low for 100-iteration solver. Oscillation builds unchecked. Fixed by switching to unitree_rl_gym gains: Kp=20, Kd=0.5. Robot now holds stable at 0.203m for 10+ seconds.
8. **Warp OOM in Python loops** — `mjx.step()` leaks GPU memory in Python loops (collision buffers not freed). Must JIT via `lax.scan`. Same 500 steps: Python loop = OOM at step 100, `lax.scan` = instant.

## Solver settings (unitree's XML, kept as-is)

## Analysis of run 4

- **Velocity tracking still broken**: cmd_vx=-0.754, actual_vx=-0.001. Robot not moving.
- **Torques NOT saturated**: max 17.6 Nm out of 45.4 Nm limit. Policy has headroom.
- **Actions ARE large**: mean abs 0.39, calves at 0.74. Policy is compensating for low Kp.
- **But it found a static asymmetric squat** instead of walking. Left side tucked, right side extended.
- **Entropy collapsed early**: 7.8 → -1.1 by 20M steps. Policy committed to squat before discovering walking.
- **entropy_coef=0.01** is too small for 12-dim actions on stiff physics. MJX gets away with it because softer solver = smoother reward landscape = easier exploration.
