# 2026-03-25 — Go2 Physics Audit & Double Damping Fix

## What we did

### Deep env audit — found double damping bug
Dispatched 3 parallel hypothesis agents. Key findings:

1. **Double damping (CONFIRMED BUG):** Go2 had effective Kd=1.0 (dof_damping=0.5 + actuator biasprm[2]=-0.5). Go1 has Kd=0.5 (dof_damping only, position actuators have biasprm[2]=0). Menagerie Go2 uses `<general biastype="affine">` actuators with built-in Kd that we never zeroed.

2. **Force limits:** Go2 calf max torque 24 Nm at 15.2 kg (ratio 1.58). Go1 calf 35.5 Nm at 12.7 kg (ratio 2.79). Go1 has 48% more calf torque per kg — real hardware difference, not a bug.

3. **Action range:** With action_scale=0.3 (MJX reference), Go2 uses only 15% of thigh range. Reverted to 0.5.

4. **Physical capability:** Manual gait test confirmed Go2 CAN lift feet (0.20m clearance vs Go1's 0.32m). Physics not the blocker.

### Damping fix — two attempts
- **Attempt 1 (seed 1200):** dof_damping=0, actuator Kd=0.5. Robot collapsed initially (eval 0.1 at 1.3M). I killed it prematurely. MISTAKE: reviewing full log shows it recovered to 10.7 at 8.7M and was climbing.
- **Attempt 2 (seed 1300):** dof_damping=0.5, actuator Kd=0. Running.

### MJX Go2 reference config — didn't help
Matched alexeiplatzer/unitree-go2-mjx-rl config (action_scale=0.3, tracking 1.5/0.8, lin_vel_z=-2.0). Seed 1100 eval 8.6 at 50M. Worse than baseline.

### Reward breakdown analysis
Confirmed robot earns reward by: yaw tracking (479) + pose (342) while failing at forward tracking (125). Feet clearance (-89) is biggest penalty. Pattern identical to Brax Go2 baseline.

## Key lesson
**Don't kill training runs prematurely.** Seed 1200 looked dead at 1.3M but was recovering at 8.7M. The dip-then-recover pattern is normal when the physics change (removed passive damping → robot needs to relearn stability).

### Menagerie go2_mjx.xml calf torque bug — THE ROOT CAUSE
Steven asked to verify Go2 calf torque against hardware specs. Agent found:
- Real Go2 calf: **45.43 Nm** (Unitree URDF)
- go2_mjx.xml: **24 Nm** (parent class default, knee doesn't override)
- Go2 calf was running at **53% of real torque**
- Go1 calf correctly set to 35.55 Nm in Menagerie

This explains everything: the robot literally couldn't generate enough force to lift
its feet. Shuffling was the physically optimal strategy at 24 Nm calf torque.

Fixed in go2_base.py. Seed 1400 running (50M steps).

### Trajectory analysis breakthrough
Saved .npz trajectories from both Brax and our PPO. Side-by-side comparison:
- **Brax**: calves at +0.7, base height 0.31m (standing tall), tracking_lin_vel=1.6
- **Ours**: calves at -1.0 (saturated), base height 0.17m (crouching), tracking_lin_vel=131.8

Both earn ~13 reward via completely different strategies. Brax stands, ours crouches.

### Network initialization investigation
Compared initial policy outputs. Brax raw means: 0.2-0.4 range. Ours: 0.02-0.03 (with lecun_uniform). Both use lecun_uniform — the difference comes from Brax's obs normalization giving different effective inputs at step 0.

Tried matching Brax obs norm (freeze during scan, batch update after). No effect — seed 1900 still crouches at 0.16m.

### Comprehensive env diff (alexeiplatzer repo)
Agent fetched and diffed alexeiplatzer/unitree-go2-mjx-rl against our env. Three smoking guns:

1. **Body collision geoms enabled** — Go1 PG uses feetonly (only foot spheres collide). Our Go2 has ALL body geoms active. At crouching pose, 4/6 contacts are spurious body-floor contacts. Robot "sits" on thighs instead of standing on feet.

2. **No height termination** — Our termination only fires on full flip. Robot can crouch to 0.17m without dying. alexeiplatzer terminates at base_z < 0.18m.

3. **Missing motor target clipping** — We don't clip targets to joint limits. alexeiplatzer does. Lower priority.

### Height termination test (seed 2000)
Added base_z < 0.18 termination. Eval LOWER (4-7 vs 12-14) — episodes die sooner but policy still crouches, just hovers above 0.18m. Height termination alone isn't enough.

### Current approach: 10x tracking reward (seed 2100)
Instead of removing contacts or adding terminations, just make walking 10x more rewarding than crouching. tracking_lin_vel=10.0, tracking_ang_vel=5.0. Crouching in pose earns ~450, but perfect tracking now earns ~4500. Running now.

### Seed 2100 result — RESOLVED
**eval 233 at 50M steps.** This is the winner.

Config: tracking_lin_vel=10.0, tracking_ang_vel=5.0, height termination (base_z < 0.18m), calf torque 45.43 Nm.

Trajectory data confirmed:
- Base height: 0.31m (standing, not crouching)
- Robot locomotes and tracks velocity commands
- Consistent with Brax's standing height (0.31m from earlier A/B trajectory data)

Root cause of the entire saga: Go1's reward weights (tracking_lin_vel=1.0) do NOT transfer to Go2. At 1.0, pose reward (~450) dominates tracking reward (~130) — crouching is the optimal strategy. At 10.0, tracking dominates and walking emerges.

The calf torque fix and height termination were necessary but not sufficient alone. All three ingredients together produce locomotion.
