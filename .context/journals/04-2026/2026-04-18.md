## Part 1 — Spawn-Bug Fix + Baseline Retrains

Yesterday's runs (before fix) had pusher spawning outside the arena walls because slide-joint `qpos` is **additive to the body's pos**, not absolute world XY. Pusher body at `pos="-0.15 ..."` + qpos=-0.19 put it at world -0.34, past the wall at ±0.3. Visible only on top-down render. Re-anchored both pusher and block bodies to origin; qpos now directly = world XY.

Retrained dense-reward baselines on fixed env (3M steps, seed 42, 256 envs per run):

| Mode | Broken env | Fixed env | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| position-PD | -89.6 | **-67.5** ± 44.5 | +22 |
| velocity (delta) | -92.1 | -90.5 ± 32.9 | +2 |
| teleport | -91.8 | -89.5 ± 32.7 | +2 |

Pos mode got a clear bump; vel/tele barely moved. Foreshadowed deeper issue for vel/tele.

## Part 2 — Reward Shaping Experiments

### v1 shaping (added to push_env.py alongside `dense`/`sparse`):

```python
r = -pos_err - 0.3*angle_err - 0.2*pusher_to_block
    + 2.0*block_vel·toward_target - 0.001*||action||² + 1.0*success
```

3M-step results:

| Mode | Dense | Shaped v1 | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| pos | -67.5 | **+143.2 ± 31.7** | **+210** 🔥 |
| vel | -90.5 | -85.5 ± 22.2 | +5 |
| tele | -89.5 | -76.3 ± 27.7 | +13 |

Pos unlocked dramatically (47% success rate per diagnostic rollout). Vel/tele barely moved.

### v2 (bumped `r_block_vel` 2→10, added `r_pusher_vel=0.5`):

| Mode | v1 | v2 |
|---|---|---|
| pos | +143 | **-1.7** (!?!) |
| vel | -85 | -55 |
| tele | -76 | -48 |

Pos _regressed_. Stronger `r_block_vel` drowned `r_angle`; policy learned to hustle the block for block_vel reward without aligning yaw to target. Success rate crashed from 47% → 3.5%. Vel/tele improved marginally but still weren't solving.

### v3 (revert `r_block_vel` to 2, keep `r_pusher_vel`):

| Mode | v1 | v3 |
|---|---|---|
| pos | +143 | -25 |
| vel | -85 | -67 |
| tele | -76 | -78 |

`r_pusher_vel` alone also hurt pos (-168 from baseline). Marginal vel gain not worth pos regression. Reverted both.

**Takeaway:** shaping term weights must stay bounded relative to ground-truth terms or policy optimizes the wrong objective. "Bigger shaping = more signal" is false beyond the boundary.

## Part 3 — Diagnostic Logging → Action-Space Root Cause

Added env-side metrics for per-component reward breakdown + velocity/proximity signals (`pusher_vel_mag`, `block_vel_mag`, `pusher_to_block`, `r_pos`, `r_angle`, `r_approach`, `r_block_vel`, `r_pusher_vel`). Ran 200-step rollouts per trained checkpoint to get mean values.

### Smoking gun

| Metric | pos_shaped | vel_shaped | tele_shaped |
|---|---|---|---|
| **pusher_vel_mag** | **1.44 m/s** | **0.06** | **0.06** |
| block_vel_mag | 0.09 | 0.009 | 0.005 |
| success rate | 47% | 0% | 0% |

Pusher velocity **24× smaller** in vel/tele despite same obs, same reward. Action caps were 2-24× larger than what policy used (7-13% of cap saturation).

### Why

- **position-PD**: `action=0 → ctrl=[0,0] ≠ current → PD yanks pusher toward origin`. Movement is the equilibrium. Policy explores aggressive actions because quiet actions still produce motion.
- **velocity/teleport**: `action=0 → ctrl=current → pusher holds still`. Stillness is the equilibrium. Any aggressive action reverts to hover next step; no local gradient favors aggression. Policy converges cautious.

Bumped scales 2-4× (vel 0.01→0.04, tele 0.25→1.5). Returned eval tele -76 → -47 (marginal), vel -85 → -55 (marginal). Structural issue, not scale issue. Documented as "zero-action attractor" — pos-PD is surprisingly the RL-friendly default for pushing, despite being jittery and unusual.

## Part 4 — gym-pusht Vendoring + Modular Rewards

Per Steven's request, stopped fighting custom env shaping and brought in the real DP benchmark.

### Attempts:
1. `pip install gym-pusht` — immediately broke on pymunk 7.2 (`Space.add_collision_handler` removed upstream).
2. Built our own `push_t_dp.py` MuJoCo port — trained but eval 0 (coverage reward too sparse for RL-from-scratch; DP uses imitation). Also had a rendering bug (missing `type="box"` → geoms rendered as spheres).
3. Vendored gym-pusht source directly.

### What shipped

- `jax_rl/envs/manipulation/pusht/pusht.py` — vendored (539 LOC + Apache 2.0 LICENSE).
- `jax_rl/envs/manipulation/pusht/pymunk_override.py` — vendored contact handler (244 LOC).
- `reward_mode` kwarg added: `"coverage"` | `"sparse"` | `"shaped"` | `"approach"`. Default matches DP. Other three unlock RL-from-scratch experiments.
- `jax_rl/envs/manipulation/pusht/demos/pusht_demos.npz` — all 206 LeRobot expert demos packed at 0.29 MB (25,650 transitions). `demos/download.py` script to refresh.
- `demos/__init__.py` — `load_demos()`, `get_episode(i)`, `n_episodes()` helpers.
- `tests/test_pusht_parity.py` — **3/3 pass**, byte-exact match vs pip gym-pusht over 100 random steps in coverage mode.

Deleted: `push_t_dp.py` (redundant, failed MuJoCo port), `push_t_jax.py` (pure-JAX port, ~500 LOC, superseded by vendored real thing).

### Demo quirk

LeRobot `next.success` flag is always False (0/206 episodes); max reward in dataset = 0.9489 (0.95 × coverage=0.901). Human teleop demos approach but don't consistently clear the DP 95% threshold. Consistent with DP paper's "expert demos are imperfect" caveat. Usable for BC / offline RL but success rate of BC-only policies will be < 100%.
