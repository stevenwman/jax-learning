# 2026-04-23 — Curriculum v11–v16 (flat transfer + 5-col curriculum)

## Context
v5 pilot (5M) showed curriculum climb. Need 20M ceiling + flat-env transfer validation. Main blocker: curriculum-trained policy collapsed in 23–34 steps on `Go2WarpJoystickFlat` (crouched stance, OOD stance height).

## What happened
- **v11/v12/v13** ablation sweep (reward weights, DR). Identified root cause: curriculum trained for goal-directed rotating body-frame cmd; flat env uses Bernoulli random cmd. Distribution mismatch → OOD behavior.
- **v13 reward tuning**:
  - `action_rate = -0.05` (was -0.1)
  - `orientation = -1.0` (was -5.0; tilted terrain was 190× penalty spike)
  - `base_height = -20.0` feet-relative (was world-frame ±0.27 target)
  - `feet_clearance = 0.0`, `feet_height = 0.0` (drop world-frame z terms)
  - Contact-based termination (`base_contact > 0`) instead of `base_z < 0.18`
- **v14 (20M, 4 cols)**: eval **294.4**, mean_level 0.71. Flat transfer still intermittent (non-deterministic across seeds). Conclusion: 4-col training insufficient for flat coverage.
- **Flat-as-5th-col hypothesis**: include flat terrain in curriculum as non-goal-directed col with Bernoulli cmd → matches distribution used by `Go2WarpJoystickFlat` eval.
- **v15 (5M smoke, 5 cols)**: clean 1000 steps on flat env, eval 285 — proved hypothesis.
- **v16 (20M, 5 cols)**: full validation. Commit 0676e47.

## v16 results
- Eval **290.3 ± 6.8** (best 294.7 @ best ckpt). Essentially tied with v14 294.4.
- mean_level per type (final): rough 0.62, pyramid_up 0.27, pyramid_down 1.00, tilted 1.04, flat 0.00 (by design).
- fall=0.00 globally.
- Videos (videos/v16/):
  - flat_L0.mp4 — survived ~600 steps, died. Non-deterministic across seeds (607/871/1000/1000).
  - pyramid_up_L1.mp4 — 70 frames, died fast (known failure mode).
  - pyramid_down_L2.mp4 — 600 frames full.
  - tilted_L3.mp4 — 196 frames, died mid-ep.
- Training: 1544 sps avg on RTX 5080, total wall 12945s (3.6h).

## Key lessons
- **legged_gym reference check**: user challenged my claim re: reward_orientation. Cloned `/tmp/legged_gym`, verified: their `reward_orientation = sum(projected_gravity[:2]²) = sin²(tilt)`. Ours `sum(torso_zaxis[:2]²)` is algebraically identical. Weight scaling (not formula) was the issue. No feature called `no_fly`. Stumble threshold is `5×` tangential, not `4×`. `feet_clearance` / `feet_height` are our project terms, not legged_gym names.
- **Warp determinism**: same seed → different lifetimes across runs. Don't rely on single-seed eval for sub-policy survival tests.
- **Flat as 5th curriculum col**: cheap and principled way to give curriculum-trained policy the Bernoulli-cmd distribution it'll see in flat evaluation. Trades 1/5 of hard-terrain training for distribution coverage.

## Still open
- pyramid_up stuck ~L0–L1 after 20M. Needs targeted fix (possibly face-stair spawn tuning, or different cmd schedule at start-of-episode).
- Flat robustness non-deterministic — policy is not robustly stable even in-distribution for all seeds.
- v16 did not decisively beat v14. Verdict: 5-col curriculum gives better distribution match but no clear win on curriculum ceiling.
