## Key realisation (corrects the redesign doc's assumption)

The doc's move #1 said "controllers are tangled in env `step()` — extract them."
**Reality: controllers were already clean strategy objects** (`Controller` ABC +
`JointPD/OSC/VarImpedance/VarImpedanceMass`, `action_size/setup/apply`) from the
2026-06-09 composition refactor. So move #1 was a **relocation**, not an
extraction — much lower risk. What was actually missing was a *home* (they shared
a 656-line file with terrain/actuation/mud) and *mud isolation*. Both now done.

The controllers are still **go2-env-coupled** (they read ~14 `env._*` internals
as a documented read-only contract), so they stay under `envs/locomotion/`. The
doc's `jax_rl/control/` promotion is deferred until they're decoupled behind a
typed contract — a separately-spec'd later move.
